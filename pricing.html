Reading the range

Why we quote a range, not a number

Condition behind the walls

Rot, outdated wiring, failed plumbing, or water damage only show up once we open things up. We price what we can see and flag what we can't.

Materials and finish level

The same layout can swing widely depending on cabinetry, tile, fixtures, and flooring. Your selections drive a large share of the total.

Permits and structure

Moving load-bearing walls, altering egress, or changing the footprint brings permitting, engineering, and inspection into the cost.

Access and site conditions

Stairs, tight lots, occupied buildings, and limited parking all affect labour hours and how the work has to be staged.

Scheduling

Work that has to happen around a business's opening hours, a tenant, or a closing date is priced differently to an empty property.

Insurance scope

On claims, the covered scope is set between you and your carrier. We document thoroughly and build to the approved scope.
